How it works

  1. Upload a photo of the mushroom you want checked.
  2. MyCoGuard matches it against a reference library of edible, inedible and poisonous species.
  3. You get a safety status, confidence score and full write-up.
  4. Download the result as a PDF report to keep or share.
⚠️ MyCoGuard is a reference tool, not a substitute for expert identification. Never eat a wild mushroom based on an app result alone.
