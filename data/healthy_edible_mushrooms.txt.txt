HEALTHY / EDIBLE MUSHROOMS

1. Button Mushroom
Uses:
- Soups
- Curries
- Stir-fries
- Pizza and pasta

Benefits:
- Provides protein
- Contains B vitamins
- Provides selenium
- Provides potassium and other minerals

Body-related effects:
- Nutrients are absorbed through the digestive system.
- Selenium contributes to normal antioxidant and cellular functions.
- B vitamins support normal energy metabolism.

Details:
Button mushroom (Agaricus bisporus) is one of the most commonly cultivated edible mushrooms. It should be obtained from reliable food sources and prepared properly.

Source:
USDA FoodData Central
NIH Office of Dietary Supplements


2. Oyster Mushroom
Uses:
- Curries
- Soups
- Stir-fries
- Rice and noodle dishes

Benefits:
- Provides protein
- Contains dietary fiber
- Provides B vitamins
- Contains minerals and bioactive compounds

Body-related effects:
- Dietary fiber supports normal digestive and bowel function.
- Nutrients are absorbed through the gastrointestinal tract.

Details:
Oyster mushroom (Pleurotus species) is a widely cultivated edible mushroom.

Source:
USDA FoodData Central


3. Shiitake Mushroom
Uses:
- Soups
- Sauces
- Stir-fries
- Noodles

Benefits:
- Provides B vitamins
- Provides copper
- Provides selenium
- Contains bioactive compounds

Body-related effects:
- Nutrients are processed through the digestive system.
- Some compounds are being researched for immune-related effects.

Details:
Shiitake (Lentinula edodes) is a popular edible mushroom used in many cuisines.

Source:
USDA FoodData Central
Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center

5. Enoki Mushroom
Uses:
- Soups
- Noodles
- Hot pots
- Stir-fries

Benefits:
- Provides dietary fiber
- Provides B vitamins
- Contains minerals

Body-related effects:
- Nutrients are absorbed through the gastrointestinal tract.

Details:
Enoki (Flammulina filiformis) is an edible mushroom. It should be cooked thoroughly because raw enoki has been associated with foodborne Listeria outbreaks.

- Soups
- Sauces
- Sautéed dishes

Benefits:

Body-related effects:
