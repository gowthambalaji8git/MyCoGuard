POISONOUS MUSHROOMS

IMPORTANT SAFETY WARNING:
Suspected mushroom poisoning can be a medical emergency.
Do not use this dataset to decide whether a wild mushroom is safe to eat.
After suspected ingestion, contact emergency medical services or a poison center immediately.


1. Death Cap — Amanita phalloides

Uses:
- No safe culinary use.

Problem / Poison:
- Can cause severe poisoning.
- Can progress to acute liver failure.

Poison type:
- Amatoxins.

How it attacks:
- Amatoxins inhibit RNA polymerase II.
- This disrupts protein synthesis in cells.

Main body targets:
- Gastrointestinal tract
- Liver
- Kidneys in severe cases

Possible symptoms:
- Severe abdominal pain
- Vomiting
- Diarrhea
- Symptoms may temporarily improve before serious liver injury develops.

Medical treatment:
- Emergency hospital treatment.
- Toxicology consultation.
- Specialist-directed supportive and antidotal treatment may be used.
- Severe liver failure may require liver-transplant evaluation.

3. Fly Agaric — Amanita muscaria

Uses:
- Not recommended as food.

Problem / Poison:
- Can cause neurological and gastrointestinal poisoning.

Poison type:
- Ibotenic acid
- Muscimol

How it attacks:
- These compounds affect neurotransmitter systems in the brain.

Main body targets:
- Central nervous system
- Gastrointestinal tract

Possible symptoms:
- Confusion
- Agitation
- Drowsiness
- Vomiting
- Hallucinations
- Seizures in severe cases

Medical treatment:
- Supportive hospital care.
- Monitoring of breathing and neurological status.
- Symptom-based treatment by medical professionals.

Source:
Merck Manual
Poison Control

- Vomiting

GENERAL MEDICAL WARNING

Mushroom poisoning can be delayed and symptoms may initially appear mild.
Do not wait for symptoms to become severe after suspected ingestion.

Do not induce vomiting unless instructed by a qualified medical professional or poison center.

For a real suspected poisoning:
1. Seek emergency medical care immediately.
2. Contact a poison center where available.
3. Keep a photograph or sample of the mushroom only if it can be collected safely.
4. Do not consume additional mushrooms for comparison.
